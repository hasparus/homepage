# Speaking

Meetup talks, mostly at [Wrocław TypeScript](https://github.com/WrocTypeScript),
where I served as a placeholder speaker.

## A tiny calendar with sync engines and CRDTs

TypeScript Fireside #4, Wrocław · May 2025 \
How I built [bear-fit](https://bear-fit.haspar.us), a calendar for finding a day
that works for all my friends. Initial experiment with val.town Townie, then a
migration to PartyKit and Yjs. \
[Code](https://github.com/hasparus/bear-fit)

## Refinement Types

What refinement types are, a research paper that adds them to TypeScript, and

[Code](https://github.com/hasparus/refinement-types-in-typescript) ·
[extended slides](https://github.com/hasparus/refinement-types-in-typescript/tree/extended-15-minutes)
· [follow-up post](/refinement-types/)

## Building a Branded Styling DSL in TypeScript

Wrocław TypeScript Online #17 · Jul 2020 \

[Recording](https://youtu.be/-0smYzH1m3s) ·
[Theme UI PR](https://github.com/system-ui/theme-ui/pull/1090)

## Progressive Web Apps Demystified

Chop-Chop · May 2020 · in Polish \

[Recording](https://youtu.be/B-XeNkjl4Ls)

## Fixing JSX.IntrinsicElements conflict in react-three-fiber

Wrocław TypeScript #12, Wrocław · Feb 2020 \
How I used the TypeScript Compiler API to generate react-three-fiber's JSX types
from three.js and stop them clashing with `@types/react`. \
[Slides](https://github.com/hasparus/r3fts) ·
[PR](https://github.com/pmndrs/react-three-fiber/pull/233)

## Maybe-ts. A softer approach to optionals

Wrocław TypeScript #10, Wrocław · Dec 2019 \
The problems with `Option` from Rich Hickey's
["Maybe Not"](https://youtu.be/YR5WdGrpoug), and a nullable `Maybe` for fp-ts
that avoids them. It turned out not to be a lawful functor. \
[Slides](https://maybe-ts.vercel.app) ·
[library](https://github.com/hasparus/maybe-ts)
